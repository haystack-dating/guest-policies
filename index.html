Privacy & data protection notice

We know dating information can be personal. This notice explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how you can control it.

1. Who we are

Haystack Dating Ltd (“Haystack”, “we”, “us”) is the data controller for the personal information described here. For privacy enquiries, data rights requests or to withdraw matching consent, email privacy@haystack-dating.com.

2. What information we collect

  • Your name, contact details, age or date of birth, and booking and attendance details.
  • Payment, transaction and refund records (usually handled through our ticketing/payment providers).
  • Gender, sexual orientation and dating preferences, including the genders of people you would like to date.
  • Answers you choose to give to event questionnaires, including interests, personality, lifestyle and relationship preferences.
  • Dietary or accessibility needs you tell us about, and correspondence, feedback, complaints or safeguarding concerns.
  • Relevant technical and website information, where collected through our online services.

3. Why we use it and our lawful bases

We use booking and contact information to provide events, handle payments, communicate with you and deliver customer support, generally where necessary for our contract with you. We use relevant information to run events and arrange groups; where appropriate, we may rely on consent, contract necessity or legitimate interests, depending on the particular purpose. We use information to meet legal and accounting obligations, and to protect guests, handle complaints and secure our services where supported by legal obligation or legitimate interests. We send marketing only where permitted by applicable law; you can opt out at any time.

4. Your separate matching consent

Some matching information, particularly sexual orientation, is special-category personal data under UK GDPR. Where we use it for group matching, we ask each attendee for separate, explicit Yes/No consent. For sexual-orientation information used in matching, we rely on explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a) UK GDPR, alongside an applicable Article 6 lawful basis for the relevant processing.

Our matching consent covers use of your gender, sexual orientation/dating preferences and profile answers to create event groups. You can say No or withdraw consent by emailing privacy@haystack-dating.com. If we cannot create an appropriate group without that information, you may be unable to participate in the matching element; any refund will depend on the circumstances and applicable booking rights. Withdrawal does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

5. How matching works

Haystack creates small, curated groups. With the relevant consent, we may consider age, dating preferences, interests, lifestyle and questionnaire answers. Our current process can involve exporting booking/questionnaire information from ticketing platforms, using AI-assisted tools to help propose groups, and reviewing or adjusting those groups ourselves. We may make manual changes for cancellations, capacity, fairness and practical event needs. A match is an introduction, not a promise of compatibility or a safety check.

We do not intend to make solely automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on guests. People remain involved in reviewing event group arrangements.

6. Who receives information

Depending on the event and service, information may be handled by ticketing providers such as Eventbrite and Fatsoma; payment providers; website, hosting, email and productivity services; AI and other technology providers (including ChatGPT Business for assisted matching); event hosts, venues and contractors where they need limited information; and professional advisers or authorities where appropriate or legally required. Providers may have their own privacy notices for services they independently control. We do not sell guest personal information.

We aim to minimise information shared, restrict access to those who need it, and avoid putting sensitive questionnaire answers on guest-facing group sheets.

7. International transfers

Some service providers may store or access information outside the UK. Where UK data protection rules require safeguards, we seek to use appropriate mechanisms, such as UK adequacy arrangements or approved contractual protections.

8. How long we keep it

Identifiable matching questionnaires and event-form information are ordinarily kept for no longer than 90 days after the relevant event, unless a shorter period applies or we have a justified reason to keep information longer, such as a legal obligation, safeguarding matter, complaint, dispute or a separately agreed use. Booking, accounting, consent and customer-service records may need different retention periods. Information that has been effectively anonymised may be retained separately.

9. Keeping information safe

We use appropriate organisational and technical safeguards and limit access according to role and need. No online service is completely risk-free. We assess and respond to suspected data breaches in line with applicable law.

10. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or transfer your information; object to certain uses; withdraw consent; and exercise rights relating to qualifying automated decisions. You have an absolute right to object to processing for direct marketing. Email privacy@haystack-dating.com and we will respond in accordance with applicable law.

11. Marketing, age and complaints

We send electronic marketing only where permitted by law. Unsubscribing from marketing does not stop essential messages about an event you booked. Haystack dating events are for adults aged 18 or over.

Contact us first if you have concerns, or complain directly to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. You do not have to contact us before approaching the ICO.

12. Updates

We review this notice as our events, technology and legal obligations change. We will update the date above and bring material changes to your attention where required.

Guest code of conduct

Haystack is here for relaxed, real-life introductions. Everyone deserves to feel welcome, comfortable and respected. By attending, guests agree to follow these ground rules.

1. Consent and boundaries come first

Respect verbal and non-verbal boundaries. Never pressure anyone for physical or sexual contact, contact details, a drink, another date or continued conversation. “No”, stepping away or asking for space must be respected immediately. Sharing a group, flirting or accepting a drink is not consent.

2. Be kind and inclusive

Harassment, threats, bullying, stalking, intimidation, unwanted sexual behaviour, discriminatory abuse and deliberately humiliating others are not acceptable.

3. Drink responsibly

Intoxication is not an excuse for misconduct. Haystack, venue staff or security may refuse service or entry or ask someone to leave where justified by safety or behaviour. Illegal drugs and unlawful conduct are prohibited.

4. Follow safety and event instructions

Follow reasonable instructions from Haystack hosts, venues and security. We may adjust groups, seating or activities where reasonably necessary for safety, wellbeing or event operations.

5. Respect everyone's privacy

Don't photograph, film or record someone who objects or where doing so would intrude on their privacy. Don't share sensitive details, private messages or personal disclosures without appropriate permission. Don't use details obtained at an event to harass, stalk or repeatedly contact someone who wants no further contact. Haystack may take event photos or video where guests have been appropriately informed and applicable permissions or opt-outs are respected.

6. Matching isn't a background check

Group placement is designed to facilitate introductions, not to guarantee anyone's identity, safety, behaviour or compatibility. Use your own judgement about further contact.

7. Speak up about concerns

If something feels unsafe or seriously uncomfortable, tell a Haystack host, venue staff or security as soon as reasonably possible. You can also report concerns afterwards through our published contact channels. In an emergency, contact emergency services. We handle reports as sensitively as reasonably possible, while sharing details where needed for safety, investigation or legal reasons.

8. What happens if someone breaks the rules

Acting reasonably and subject to applicable law and booking rights, we may change a guest's group, refuse entry, end participation, require someone to leave or restrict future attendance for serious or repeated misconduct or safety concerns. We may involve authorities where appropriate.

9. Be genuine

Provide materially accurate booking and matching information, meet event age and eligibility rules, and don't deliberately misrepresent your identity or dating preferences in a way that undermines other guests' experience.

Questions? We're here.

For privacy requests, matching-consent withdrawals or questions about how we handle information, email privacy@haystack-dating.com.

For general event enquiries or to report an event concern, use Haystack's normal customer-service channels on our website.
